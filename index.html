How I work

I start with
the problem.

At Oracle, Bounce, Zerohash, and Vertical Raise, I learned how to build inside established teams. Running VectorScale Labs has also put me on the other side of those decisions, choosing what to build with a small team and listening to the people who use it.

I start by asking who has the problem, what they do today, and what would show that we’ve improved things. That gives us a way to decide what to build first and where the engineering effort is worth it.
